# Icon licence

The PNG files in `icons/` are rendered from the **Noto Color Emoji** font
(Google, https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji) by `tools/render-icons.py`:
country flags, the ghost (connected/disconnected), the hourglass (busy) and the
warning sign (error). `ghost-off.png` is a greyscale derivative.

Noto Emoji images are licensed under the **Apache License, Version 2.0**
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0); the font itself is under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Flag images are in the public domain where applicable.
